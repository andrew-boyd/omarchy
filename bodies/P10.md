# [Intel Mac P10] Consolidate Broadcom calibration and sleep recovery

Combine omacom/omarchy#11017's MacBookPro13,3 / 106b:015a calibration, omacom/omarchy#7671's MacBookPro14,2/14,3 path and omacom/omarchy#12314's pre-sleep unbind/post-wake rebind.
