# [Intel Mac P10] Consolidate Broadcom calibration and sleep recovery

Combine the selected Broadcom calibration work with omacom/omarchy#12314’s pre-sleep/post-wake recovery. Software follow-ups preserve customized hooks, serialize recovery, retain failed rebind records and keep pending devices across repeated pre-sleep calls. The selected model/driver policy is unchanged.
